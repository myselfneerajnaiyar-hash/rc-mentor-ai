Which of the following can be most reasonably inferred?
PASSAGE DETAILRegular attenders did better; more high scorers chose to attend.
ASeminar benefits were concentrated among high scorers.×
BMaking the seminar compulsory would raise performance.×
CStronger preparation may partly explain the better results.✓
DThe seminar's format practice mainly raised scores.×
UNSUPPORTED INFERENCE
WHY IT FAILED

The passage does not say the seminar's benefits were limited to high scorers.

WHAT YOU MISSED

High scorers and seminar benefits both appear in the passage. That match alone does not prove the claim.

WHAT TO DO NEXT TIME

Keep the conclusion within what the passage supports. Plausibility alone is not evidence.
