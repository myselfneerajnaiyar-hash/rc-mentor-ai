# Storyboard — One Continuous Construction Move

| Time | Building action | Camera / type |
|---|---|---|
| 0–3s | Empty surveyed site | Low forward push; restrained RC hook |
| 2–7s | Rebar grid lays in, concrete foundation pours in sections | Camera closes on slab; FOUNDATION / TOPIC |
| 7–14s | Column cages rise, concrete pillars grow floor by floor | Camera travels between columns and tracks upward; PILLARS / PARAGRAPHS |
| 13–21s | Beams slide from the sides and lock across the frame | Camera follows the beam path; BUT / HOWEVER / THEREFORE / INSTEAD |
| 18–29s | Floor plate, wall panels, glazing and room details form | Camera passes through the interior; EVIDENCE / EXAMPLES |
| 28–34s | Rafters connect, roof panels lower into place and close | Camera rises with the roof; AUTHOR’S MAIN POINT / ROOF |
| 34–40s | Final trim completes; camera pulls up and out to reveal the home | Brief mapping, then conceptual end line and Auctor mark |

Construction pieces retain their identity through the shot. Every animated
state is computed from the same composition time so arbitrary-frame seeking
reproduces the same build and camera pose.
