# Auctor VARC Bootcamp — final cinematic ad

## Creative rebuild

The earlier renders leaned on long, full-screen product captures with slow crop moves and repeated layouts. The product text was too small to read in several frames, and the story emphasized the tour of the interface more than the reasoning behind a wrong answer.

This version opens on the mistake, then animates the verified sample inference question from the public Bootcamp preview. The exact selected answer A is crossed out, the supported answer C is confirmed, and the error is named as an unsupported inference, matching the genuine answer rationale and Birbal feedback. The ad then moves into a three-beat explanation and a single connected sequence of the verified warm-up, RC, VA, review, and next-session steps.

The supplied Yash reference at `C:\Users\NERAJ\OneDrive\Desktop\Auctor creatives\yash.mp4` was accessible and reviewed. It uses a dark field, concise copy, and product evidence that stays close to the message. The final composition retains that message-to-proof relationship while using Auctor's navy/orange identity, a distinct story, and more active answer-level motion.

## Accuracy and assets

- The selected/correct answer state and Birbal panel are genuine captures from the Auctor public preview.
- The answer sequence is an editorial visualization of the exact sample question and answer choices, with its wording checked against the genuine review capture.
- The error label “Unsupported inference” is supported by the actual Birbal review.
- The daily activity sequence is limited to stages present in the Bootcamp interface/program structure.
- No duration, pricing, score improvement, testimonial, outcome guarantee, or persistent personalized-history claim is included.
- The underscore is generated by the included `composition/render-score.mjs` script; no third-party track or sample is used. There is no voiceover.
