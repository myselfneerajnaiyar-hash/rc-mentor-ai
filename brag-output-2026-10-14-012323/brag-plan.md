# Brag Plan: Auctor CAT VARC Bootcamp

## What is this app?
Auctor's CAT VARC Bootcamp is a 45-day guided practice program with daily warm-up, reading-comprehension, verbal-ability and review activities.

## The angle
“Don't study VARC. Train VARC.” Start with a question CAT aspirants recognize, then reveal the real practice loop and how its review connects the text, answer and reasoning.

## Hook
Deep navy, immediate editorial type: “YOU DON'T NEED / ANOTHER VARC LECTURE.” A quiet tick gives the first line room before the second arrives.

## Key moments
- A real published Day 1 RC passage and its question/options, captured from the public preview.
- The actual mission, with the five activity steps visible, followed by the real answer-review screen.
- The verified curriculum counts appear as plan structure, never as a student's score or outcome.

## Outro
“Stop preparing passively. Start training for CAT VARC.” Auctor logo, “Start your training,” and the known public route close the film.

## User flow worth showing
Published preview mission → timed RC question → answer and evidence review. The preview uses published content and does not create or alter student progress.

## Tone
- Preset: polished / cinematic
- Creative direction: premium editorial product film with quiet tension, authentic interface footage and restrained orange highlights.
- Interpretation: big type and patient holds at the opening; the actual app carries the middle; clean, confident closing card.

## Format: vertical — 1080×1920
## Duration: 40 seconds

## Visual identity
- Background: #07111d, sampled against the live Bootcamp app palette
- Accent: #f5a549, restrained mint #a9e9d2 for actual UI details
- Text: #f2efe7
- Display font: Georgia
- Body font: Arial / system sans-serif
- Strongest visual element: genuine mobile screenshots from the public Bootcamp Day 1 preview.

## Product proof
The app defines a 45-day plan with 3 RC passages per day, 25 daily questions and 8 VA questions per day. The plan totals resolve to 135 passages, 1,125 questions and 360 VA questions. These are curriculum counts, not performance results. Current live price could not be fetched from this environment and is omitted.

## Audio direction
- Role: original, restrained cinematic underscore with sparse synthetic ticks and a soft resolving tone.
- Music: original synthesized score generated for this composition; no third-party music.
- Music treatment: slow harmonic bed, muted pulse under the question and training sequence, gentle fade at the end.
- Voice: the exact supplied script is retained as timed on-screen narration captions. No local speech engine was available: Hyperframes TTS requires Python/Kokoro, Python is unavailable, and the Windows speech-to-file path was denied by the environment.
- SFX posture: only a few quiet, original tonal accents inside the score.
- Restraint rule: keep all sound below the captions' reading pace; no dramatic impacts or fake ticking timer.

Transition mood: dissolve → curriculum scale.

Sequential/interaction: counters settle one by one and then hold together.
Audio intent: quiet harmonic lift.
Transition mood: decisive → closing card.

The real Auctor mark, VARC Bootcamp, exact final narration, CTA “START YOUR TRAINING,” and `rc.auctorlabs.in/bootcamp-2026`. No price is shown because production could not be fetched for live price verification.

Audio intent: resolve and fade cleanly.
