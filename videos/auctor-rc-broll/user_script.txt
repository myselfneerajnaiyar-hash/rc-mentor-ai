Create a premium 10–15 second vertical Reel-format product showcase clip for Auctor RC.

PURPOSE:
This is NOT a standalone advertisement.
It is reusable B-roll that will be inserted into multiple Instagram/Reels videos whenever the narration talks about Auctor diagnosing mistakes, understanding performance, and giving an improvement plan.

CORE IDEA:
"Your personal VARC trainer doesn't just tell you whether you are right or wrong — Auctor shows you WHERE you are going wrong and WHAT you should improve."

FORMAT:
- 1080 × 1920
- 30 fps
- 10–15 seconds
- Premium, cinematic, modern EdTech aesthetic
- Full vertical composition
- The visual must use the entire frame.
- Do NOT leave the bottom half empty.
- No voiceover.
- Add a subtle inspirational/modern tech music bed if appropriate, but keep it usable underneath future voiceovers. Music must be low enough that narration can easily be layered later.

IMPORTANT CREATIVE DIRECTION:
Do NOT make this a sequence of giant screenshots.
Do NOT simply place a screen recording inside a phone frame and leave it static.

Instead, create the illusion that a student is actively using Auctor on a premium smartphone.

The phone should be the HERO visual.

Use:
- realistic premium smartphone/device frame
- slight 3D perspective
- subtle hand/finger interaction if possible
- natural phone movement
- UI transitions inside the phone
- close-ups / push-ins into important UI elements
- small floating UI elements outside the phone when useful
- subtle depth, glow, shadows and motion
- polished transitions

The overall feeling should be:
"Someone is actually using Auctor and the platform is actively understanding their performance."

STORY / SEQUENCE:

0–2 sec:
Start with a premium smartphone entering the frame.

Above/behind the phone, display:

AUCTOR RC

Then immediately introduce the first concept:

DIAGNOSIS

The phone screen shows an authentic Auctor RC question/review interface.

A student has selected an incorrect answer.

Animate:
WRONG ANSWER → red X

Then zoom into the diagnosis.

A small diagnostic label appears dynamically, for example:

UNSUPPORTED INFERENCE

or another authentic diagnosis/category available in the existing Auctor UI.

The important thing is that the viewer sees:
Auctor doesn't merely say "Wrong."
It identifies the TYPE OF MISTAKE.

2–6 sec:
Transition smoothly inside the phone to:

ANALYTICS

Show the authentic Auctor analytics/performance interface.

Do NOT show the entire dashboard as a static screenshot.

Instead:
- animate into one meaningful metric
- highlight a pattern
- show a graph/metric moving or being selected
- use a cursor/tap/focus effect
- briefly show that the system has accumulated the student's performance history

The viewer should understand:
"Auctor knows my patterns."

6–10 sec:
Transition to:

IMPROVEMENT

Inside the phone, show authentic Auctor/Birbal feedback or improvement-oriented UI.

The visual should communicate:

PROBLEM → DIAGNOSIS → WHAT TO DO NEXT

If the existing product UI contains feedback such as:
- what went wrong
- why the option was wrong
- how to improve
- Birbal explanation

use those authentic elements.

Do NOT invent fake product functionality.

Use small animated callouts to draw attention to the important part of the interface.

10–13/15 sec:
Bring the phone slightly forward / center.

The three concepts appear sequentially around it:

DIAGNOSIS
↓
ANALYTICS
↓
IMPROVEMENT

Then finish with:

AUCTOR RC

Small supporting line:

Know your mistakes.
Know how to improve.

Keep the final frame clean because this clip will be embedded inside other Reels.
