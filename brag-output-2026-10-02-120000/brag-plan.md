# Brag Plan: Auctor Labs

## What is this app?
Auctor Labs trains CAT VARC reading through daily RC practice, focused workouts, vocabulary work and Birbal coaching.

## The angle
Reading speed is not the whole problem: understanding is built in the small daily loop of practice, analysis and improvement. The film starts with pressure in a dark reading space, then reveals Auctor and moves through faithful reconstructions of the real RC Arena, Daily Workout, Vocabulary Lab and Birbal screens. The product screens are based on current app component copy and visual conventions; any small answer-choice/demo details are illustrative, with no learner data or private content.

## Hook (first 2-3 seconds)
Dark page-like lines compress and fall out of focus under a narrow reading light. The line “Reading isn't the problem. Understanding is.” resolves in two deliberate beats.

## Key moments (the middle)
- Auctor Labs mark reveals through a fine blue-white light sweep.
- RC Arena: a passage, question and evidence-led answer choices, based on the existing dark RC practice card language.
- Today's 30-Min Workout: Speed Drill, Vocabulary, 2 RC Passages and Tone / Main Idea, using the exact labels present in `components/HomeView.jsx`.
- Vocabulary Lab: its actual title, revision copy, flow labels and the “Obscure” entry from `app/data/vocabLessons.js`.
- Birbal: actual avatar and “Birbal's Coaching Plan” / Strength / Weakness structure from `components/BirbalCoachCard.jsx`.

## Outro / punchline
Three beats: “Practice.” “Analyse.” “Improve.” Final wordmark and “Train for CAT VARC. Every day.” with `www.auctorlabs.in`.

## User flow worth showing
Choose the CAT RC Arena or a focused daily workout → work through vocabulary and passages → review coaching guidance from Birbal.

## Tone
- Preset: cinematic
- Creative direction: premium technology commercial, grounded in actual student-facing study screens
- Interpretation: quiet pressure and restrained light at the opening; measured logo reveal; confident moving UI screens; rhythmic but readable word montage; decisive branded end card.

## Format: vertical — 1080x1920
## Duration: 20 seconds

## Visual identity (from the project)
- Background: `#0F172A` / slate-900 with darker slate surfaces
- Accent: blue and indigo from the app; restrained cyan light detail
- Text: `#E2E8F0` / slate-200, white for primary headlines
- Display font: Georgia for cinematic hook; Segoe UI for wordmark and app UI
- Body font: system UI / Segoe UI, matching the product's sans-serif interface
- Strongest visual element: the dark Auctor study interface, indigo workout panel, and Birbal avatar.

## Share copy (draft)
Auctor Labs: train for CAT VARC through practice, analysis and a little more understanding every day.

## Audio direction
- Role: cinematic support with a custom locally synthesized score and restrained UI accents
- Music: original 20-second tonal score generated locally from sine oscillators; no third-party song or paid asset
- Music treatment: low, warm pulse begins under the hook; chord lift on the brand reveal; understated rhythmic movement through feature screens; soft tail beneath the end card
- Music cue guidance: custom audio has no preset; reveals land at the storyboard beats (3s, 6s, 14s and 18s), with no forced external beat-grid claims
- Audio-reactive treatment: subtle manually timed breathing in the blue light layers; no waveform or equalizer graphics
- SFX posture: sparse; generated, gentle low impact and soft UI ticks at scene transitions
- Audio-coupled moments: one low opening hit; soft mark reveal at 3s; restrained taps as app screens change; final low resolved note at 18s
- Restraint rule: no exaggerated trailer booms or synthetic voiceover.
