# Composition brief

## Audience and message

CAT 2026 VARC learners who practice but do not know why an answer failed. Show the product as daily guided practice: a wrong answer is explained, corrected, and carried as a prior review into later work.

## Visual system

- Portrait 1080 × 1920, 30 fps, 45 seconds.
- Auctor dark navy and orange with restrained white, red for the rejected answer, and green for the correct answer.
- Dimensional card surfaces, deliberate depth, sequential entrance and story-motivated travel. No full-page screenshots as scene backgrounds.
- Real product copy, real Birbal artwork, real brand logo and a real score from the existing video assets.

## Content provenance

- Diagnostic question, passage, options A–D, correct answer C, and the explanation for A are reproduced from the Auctor answer-review capture.
- The three Birbal feedback beats use the actual answer-review rationale, trap explanation, and next-time guidance.
- The new question uses the actual `Question 1 of 4 · Main Idea` question prompt and an excerpt from the source passage visible in Auctor’s RC question capture.
- The session loop uses the Bootcamp's verified 5 warm-up + 12 RC + 8 VA structure, followed by review and a next session. No performance data or fake dashboard is shown.
- `PREVIOUS REVIEW` labels the diagnosis card as a carried review. It does not claim one mistake proves a persistent weakness.

## Sound

Existing instrumental score, time-stretched to the requested duration with an ending fade. Quiet interface accents mark the selection, rejection and memory-card docking. No voiceover.

## Editable source

`composition/index.html` is the Hyperframes composition. All choreography is authored as HTML/CSS and a GSAP timeline; source assets live under `composition/assets/`.
