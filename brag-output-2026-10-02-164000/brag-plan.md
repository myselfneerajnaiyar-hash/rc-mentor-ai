# Brag Plan: Auctor Labs

## What is this app?
Auctor Labs is a student-facing CAT VARC practice app with reading comprehension, daily workouts, vocabulary, and Birbal guidance.

## The angle
Reading is only the start: the film moves from the pressure of comprehension into a real student app journey, then lands on a daily practice promise.

## Hook
Dark editorial pressure, paper grain, and “Reading isn't the problem. Understanding is.”

## Key moments
- Real RC practice UI.
- Daily Workout and vocabulary screens, moving like a focused mobile product film.
- Birbal guidance as the human-feeling close to the learning loop.

## Outro / punchline
“Train for CAT VARC. Every day.” Auctor Labs identity and auctor.in.

## User flow worth showing
Open RC practice → build a daily workout and vocabulary habit → get Birbal guidance.

## Tone
- Preset: cinematic
- Creative direction: premium, high-contrast technology commercial
- Interpretation: considered pacing, sharp typography, elegant UI movement, restrained sound.

## Format: vertical — 1080x1920
## Duration: 20 seconds

## Visual identity
- Background: deep ink navy/black
- Accent: Auctor indigo, sampled from the product's verified mobile captures
- Text: warm ivory
- Display font: locally installed Georgia/Arial fallback
- Body font: system sans-serif
- Strongest visual element: actual Auctor student app screenshots.

## Share copy
Auctor Labs. Train for CAT VARC. Every day.

## Audio direction
- Role: cinematic support with restrained UI accents
- Music: bundled Ende “Happy Beats / Business Moves” vol. 10
- Music treatment: low opening, fuller product reveal, fade on final brand card.
- Music cue guidance: local vol. 10 cue preset; strong accents near 3.01, 8.73, 15.82, and 18.55 seconds. Readability takes priority over beat snapping.
- Audio-reactive treatment: subtle glow breathing.
- SFX posture: minimal, premium motion-matched clicks and low reveal hit.
- Audio-coupled moments: brand reveal, screen transitions, three-word montage.
- Restraint rule: no voiceover, no oversized risers, no constant impacts.

## Storyboard
### Scene 1 — The pressure — 3s
Dark cinematic opening, refined typographic reveal: “Reading isn't the problem. Understanding is.” No stock footage.
Sequential/interaction: sentence reveals in two deliberate phrases.
Audio intent: low, anticipatory.
Transition mood: dramatic → Scene 2

### Scene 2 — Brand reveal — 3s
Auctor Labs logo and name appear with a restrained light sweep.
Sequential/interaction: mark, then name.
Audio intent: warm tonal reveal.
Transition mood: clean → Scene 3

### Scene 3 — Real student product — 8s
Use the actual verified RC, Daily Workout, Vocabulary, and Birbal captures in a moving phone composition with readable crop and labels.
Sequential/interaction: screen-to-screen transitions in the stated order.
Audio intent: confident pulse and soft interface accents.
Transition mood: rhythmic → Scene 4

### Scene 4 — Three-beat montage — 4s
Full-screen “Practice.” “Analyse.” “Improve.” with quick cinematic transitions.
Sequential/interaction: one word at a time, each held for reading.
Audio intent: three restrained accents.
Transition mood: dramatic → Scene 5

### Scene 5 — End card — 2s
“Train for CAT VARC. Every day.” Auctor Labs logo and auctor.in.
Sequential/interaction: brand locks, then line and URL settle.
Audio intent: resolve and fade.
Transition mood: soft.

**Music mood:** cinematic, polished
**Audio summary:** a soft opening develops into a confident pulse and resolves under the end card.
