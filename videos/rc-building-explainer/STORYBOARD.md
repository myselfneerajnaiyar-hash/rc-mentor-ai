## Video direction

- Palette: use the adopted Auctor palette in `frame.md` — light blue-gray ground, deep navy structure and text, soft cyan for active/highlighted structure. Restrained, premium, high contrast.
- Motion grammar: one blueprint-to-building visual language; precise architectural line drawing, physical blocks that lock into place, then a controlled camera pullback. Each frame reveals in timed beats across its full duration. Compose bespoke scenes; use `svg-path-draw` for blueprint/building strokes and `dynamic-content-sequencing` for staged text/block reveals. Smooth, deliberate, no idle loops.
- Stillness allocation: hold briefly after the building is complete in the hook, after the full argument model lands, and on the final mnemonic for legibility.
- Negative list: no stock footage, people, classroom, generic interface, invented passage text, full-page passage screenshot, decorative floating shapes, voiceover, music, SFX, slideshow front-loading, or screensaver motion.
- Content rule: quoted passage text below is copied exactly from the provided source. Labels are functional analysis labels requested in the brief, not quotations. Use excerpts only at their relevant beat.
