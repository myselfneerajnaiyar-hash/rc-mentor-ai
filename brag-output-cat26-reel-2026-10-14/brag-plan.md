# Brag Plan: Auctor CAT 26 VARC Bootcamp

## What is this app?
Auctor's guided VARC Bootcamp gives CAT students a daily warm-up, RC and VA practice, followed by review with Birbal.

## The angle
Mock after mock can leave the same reasoning mistake untouched. The reel turns one authentic, incorrect inference into a connected answer-to-diagnosis-to-next-step sequence, then expands that into the Bootcamp's daily practice. The cinematic urgency belongs to the calendar; the product proof is the real question, option rationale and Bootcamp flow.

## Hook (first 2-3 seconds)
A nearly full-height clock snaps on. A giant August-to-November calendar flips forward as CAT 26 approaches.

## Key moments (the middle)
- Five explicitly illustrative mock-score cards crowd the full canvas, then freeze and clear for an authentic question.
- Four real options reveal separately. A is selected, struck through in red and labeled `UNSUPPORTED INFERENCE`; C is confirmed in green. Birbal's actual rationale stays connected to the answer.
- Birbal enters as a cutout beside a clearly illustrative history timeline. The timeline resolves into the real daily activity path: warm-up, RC, VA, review and next session.

## Outro / punchline
The path contracts into the Auctor mark. `ONE SESSION. EVERY DAY.` and `ONE MISSION. CAT 26.` lead to the simple training CTA and site URL.

## User flow worth showing
Open a guided session → answer RC questions and VA practice → review the response with Birbal and continue to the next mission.

## Tone
- Preset: cinematic
- Creative direction: premium full-height CAT countdown advertisement
- Interpretation: urgent opening and layered motion; deliberate holds around the answer, diagnosis and CTA. Navy, orange, authentic UI and a restrained inspirational music bed preserve Auctor identity.

## Format: vertical — 1080x1920
## Duration: 45 seconds

## Visual identity (from the project)
- Background: #07111d / deep navy
- Accent: #f4a04f / Auctor orange
- Text: #f7f5ef / warm white
- Display font: Georgia for editorial emphasis; Arial/Helvetica for interface labels
- Body font: Arial/Helvetica
- Strongest visual element: the real answer-review interface and Birbal rationale

## Share copy (draft)
CAT is getting closer. Auctor VARC Bootcamp turns daily practice into review with Birbal, one session at a time.

## Audio direction
- Role: cinematic support
- Music: existing 45-second Auctor score from the previous approved local composition
- Music treatment: begin under the clock, build through mock cards, open at diagnosis and brand reveal, finish cleanly on CTA
- Music cue guidance: use natural scene timing; cue map unavailable for this existing score
- Audio-reactive treatment: none; keep readable text stable
- SFX posture: sparse, motion matched
- Audio-coupled moments: clock ticks, mock-card arrivals, red cross, diagnosis and brand landing
- Restraint rule: no voiceover; no sound effects that compete with the rationale

## Storyboard

### Scene 1 — The calendar — 4.8s
A large ticking dial and calendar pages fill the vertical frame. AUG, SEP, OCT, NOV flip forward into `CAT 26 / AROUND THE CORNER.`
Sequential/interaction: yes — months and dates advance one at a time.
Audio intent: begin with restrained tension; ticks follow the clock.
Audio-coupled idea: page flips build with the score.
Transition mood: date zoom becomes a score tile → Scene 2

### Scene 2 — Mock after mock — 4.7s
Five mock cards with the supplied scores 62, 71, 68, 74, 69 enter from different depths. A persistent `ILLUSTRATIVE MOCK SCORES` label makes clear these are ad graphics. Cards stack, crowd and freeze at STOP.
Sequential/interaction: yes — one card at a time, then a freeze.
Audio intent: build a measured pulse, then cut the motion on STOP.
Audio-coupled idea: card arrivals accent the rhythm.
Transition mood: hard freeze / score card opens into a real question → Scene 3

### Scene 3 — The question — 7.0s
Recreate the exact sample inference question and four authentic answer options as large, separate rows. Reveal A–D sequentially. Select A after all options are visible; maintain its text and the prompt for context.
Sequential/interaction: yes — four rows reveal independently; A is then selected.
Audio intent: lower intensity so the viewer can read.
Audio-coupled idea: soft row arrivals and one selection click.
Transition mood: red cross draws from the selected row → Scene 4

### Scene 4 — Why wrong — 7.0s
Strike A with a prominent red cross; attach the genuine diagnosis `UNSUPPORTED INFERENCE`; confirm C with a green tick. Show Birbal's three actual explanation fragments, highlighting the relevant phrase in each beat: `WHY IT FAILS` → `THE EVIDENCE` → `WHAT TO DO NEXT TIME`. Keep answer, diagnosis and feedback on one connected visual path.
Sequential/interaction: yes — red cross, diagnosis, correct answer, then the three feedback beats.
Audio intent: one decisive impact, then score opens slightly.
Audio-coupled idea: cross and diagnosis arrival, then three readable held feedback beats.
Transition mood: answer lines travel toward Birbal → Scene 5

### Scene 5 — Trainer and history — 5.0s
Birbal enters as a transparent character cutout. Beside him, an `ILLUSTRATIVE HISTORY` timeline shows the same authored label recurring on sample days, then a final correction. It is an explanatory graphic, not a real student's record.
Sequential/interaction: yes — Day 01, 04, 09, 15 and resolved state.
Audio intent: warm, confident turn.
Audio-coupled idea: timeline dots light up in sequence.
Transition mood: history path contracts to the brand mark → Scene 6

### Scene 6 — Product reveal — 3.0s
The Auctor mark and `CAT 26 / VARC BOOTCAMP` arrive as a clean cinematic reveal.
Sequential/interaction: yes — mark, then product name.
Audio intent: strongest musical lift to date.
Audio-coupled idea: one logo impact.
Transition mood: logo geometry extends into a vertical training path → Scene 7

### Scene 7 — Daily training path — 7.0s
Animate large connected blocks for WARM-UP (5 questions), RC (12 questions), VA (8 questions), REVIEW and NEXT MISSION. Use the real activity-rail crop briefly as product proof; individual blocks remain the dominant moving elements.
Sequential/interaction: yes — blocks light up in order and flow into the next.
Audio intent: forward-moving and clear.
Audio-coupled idea: restrained ticks on block arrivals.
Transition mood: path rises into the CTA frame → Scene 8

### Scene 8 — One mission — 6.5s
Promise: `YOU SHOW UP. ONE SESSION EVERY DAY.` Then the restrained end card: Auctor mark, `CAT 26 / VARC BOOTCAMP`, `START YOUR TRAINING`, `auctorlabs.in`. Keep all key copy in the Reels-safe middle area.
Sequential/interaction: yes — message first, then brand and CTA.
Audio intent: confident resolve, clean end.
Audio-coupled idea: score settles; no extra impact under URL.
Transition mood: hold to end
