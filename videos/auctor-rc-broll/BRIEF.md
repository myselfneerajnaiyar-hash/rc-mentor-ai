## Intent

Create a premium, reusable 10–15 second vertical B-roll product showcase for
Auctor RC. This is not a standalone advertisement; it will sit underneath
future narration in multiple Reels. The product should feel actively used and
understanding a learner's performance.

## Customizations

- Premium smartphone is the hero, with a subtle 3D angle, entry move, tap ripple, and internal UI transitions.
- Use a quiet local instrumental bed at low volume so narration can be layered later. No voiceover.
- Label the recreated Reading IQ chart `SAMPLE PROFILE` so representative points are not presented as a real student's history.
- Keep all important text above the bottom caption-safe band and the whole 1080×1920 canvas intentionally composed.
